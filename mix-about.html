Our storyJust added

Jeff and Barbara White own Jeff's Junk together, and it has been a family business serving Barrie since 1992.

Since 2016 alone, more than 11,000 customers have booked us, from a single couch to a whole house, a driveway bin to a job site. Every one of them gets the same full, professional service.

We recycle or donate what we can. As the logistics partner of Furniture Bank, we pick up gently used furniture and get it to families who need it.
