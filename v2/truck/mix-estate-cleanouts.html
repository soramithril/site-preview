Clearing out a parent's house

It usually comes at one of three times.

Whichever it is, you tell us what is staying and we do the heavy part.

A parent moving into careWhen Mom or Dad moves into a retirement home or long term care, most of the house cannot go with them. Once the family has taken what it is keeping, we clear out the rest. Planned around the move date.
Clearing a home after a lossClearing a loved one's home is hard, and it often falls to one or two people. Take the time you need to go through it. When you are ready, one call clears the house, basement, garage and shed.
Downsizing to a smaller placeMoving to a condo or a smaller home means letting go of a lot. Take what fits, and we clear out what is left, so the old place is ready to sell. Worked around the house sale, any room, any stairs.

Had an estate sale? We clear out whatever did not sell. Executors, realtors and landlords call us too: one call for a clear house, ready to list, show or rent.

Where it all goes

Not everything belongs at the dump.

Here is what happens to it.

What the family keepsTell us what is staying and we work around it.
Donated: good furnitureGoes to families in need through Furniture Bank, where we can, with a charitable tax receipt. Good mattresses too. Like all our donation pickups, there is a fee.
Recycled: appliances and moreFridges, stoves, washers and dryers go to recycling, and so does anything else that can be.
Hauled: everything elseLoaded and hauled away by our crew, so the house is empty and ready.
