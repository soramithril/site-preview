Which bin size should I rent?

Most homeowners go with our 14 yard bin. It's the right size for a garage or basement clean out, a small reno or a move. Some people upgrade to our 20 yard bin, the one our contractors use most for big jobs.

Digging up the garden or breaking up an old patio? Our 4 and 7 yard bins are made for dirt and concrete. Still not sure? Tell us what you're clearing out and we'll help you pick. Our bin size guide has the details.

How long can I keep my bin?

As long as you need it. You choose the pickup day when you book, and we come and get the bin that day.

Finished early? Give us a call and we'll pick it up sooner. Need more time? You can extend your rental for a fee. Long term rentals are available too.

How can I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Visa Debit and e-Transfer.

For bin rentals, we ask for a deposit when you book, starting at $450. The amount depends on your location, and we'll confirm it when we call.
