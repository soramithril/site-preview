Junk removal or a bin rental?

If you can load it yourself, a bin is often cheaper: the 14 yard bin is $195 for 7 days in Zone 1, plus the dump fee. If the junk is heavy or awkward, or you'd rather not lift it, our crew does the sorting, lifting and loading for you.
