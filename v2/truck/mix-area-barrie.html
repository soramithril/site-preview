Barrie's permit rule

A bin on your driveway or the road.

A bin fully on your own driveway normally needs no City permit. Anything on the road, boulevard or sidewalk does.

  1. 1
    On your drivewayThe usual spot. Wood goes under the bin so it does not mark the surface.
  2. 2
    Where City land startsThe City can own land up to 3 metres back from the sidewalk, so the end of your driveway may be City land. Check your survey.
  3. 3
    On the road, boulevard or sidewalkYou need the City's Right of Way Activity permit, which lists "Disposal Bin" by name. Apply online and allow 5 business days. The City asks for $2 million liability insurance. Getting it is up to you.
