Hours and holidays

When the dump is open.

  • Waste & Recycling Depot: Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 am to 3:30 pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.
  • Household Hazardous Waste Facility: Saturdays, 8:30 am to 3:30 pm.
  • The Recycling Depot at the bottom of the hill opens about April to late November.
  • Closed Christmas Day, Boxing Day, New Year's Day, Good Friday, Canada Day and September 30. Closes at noon on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

What they won't take.

AsbestosThe landfill refuses it.
Insulation that isn't baggedBagged insulation has its own fee. Loose insulation is refused.
SoilTopsoil and clean fill are refused. Dirt goes in our dirt and concrete bins: NO MIXING, one material per bin, no exceptions.

Not a Barrie resident?

The Barrie landfill is for Barrie residents only. Innisfil uses the County of Simcoe drop offs. Orillia has its own site on Kitchener Street. See our Innisfil and Orillia pages.

The curb won't take it either.

The City doesn't collect furniture, appliances or renovation waste at the curb. Extra garbage bags need a City tag.

When it's cheaper to call us.

If you have a truck or a trailer and a small load, the dump is cheaper. Call us when there's no truck, the load is big or heavy, or you'd rather not sort it at the scale: an unsorted load costs almost twice the garbage rate. See junk removal in Barrie or bin rental in Barrie.

Can I get compost at the Barrie landfill?

Yes. Compost and mulch made from Barrie's yard waste is sold to residents: $5 for a small load, $10 for a large one.

What's the Barrie landfill phone number?

The Barrie Environmental Centre is 705-739-4219.

Does the Barrie landfill take hazardous waste?

Yes, free for residents, on Saturdays at the Household Hazardous Waste Facility.

Is it cheaper to go to the dump or hire junk removal?

For a small load in your own truck, the dump. For a big, heavy or unsorted load, or no truck, call us. Junk removal starts at $125.
