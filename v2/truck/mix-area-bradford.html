A bin on a Bradford road

The Town says yes, you need a permit.

Most bins go on the driveway, with wood underneath. On a Town road, the answer is written down.

  1. 1
    Apply earlyThe Town's Road Occupancy and Entrance Permits page lists "Renovation/garbage bin" as YES. Apply for the Road Occupancy Permit at least 10 business days before the bin goes out, through the Town's online application. Getting it is up to you.
  2. 2
    On your drivewayNo road, no Town permit. It is where most bins go.
  3. 3
    County roadsSome roads in Bradford West Gwillimbury belong to the County, like County Roads 27 and 88 at Bond Head. The Town's permit does not cover them: call the County first.

Town of Bradford West Gwillimbury, Road Occupancy Permits page, checked October 2026.

The Bradford dump on Line 12

What our truck cannot take.

The County's West Gwillimbury drop off is in Bradford West Gwillimbury itself, about 15 minutes from downtown Bradford. It is also where the things our truck can't take should go.

Not usPropane tanks. Tires. Paint, oils and chemicals.
Line 12A household hazardous waste depot. Tires, up to 10 free. Propane tanks.
WhereWest Gwillimbury drop off and Household Hazardous Waste Depot, 2960 Line 12.
WhoCounty of Simcoe residents, with proof of where the waste comes from.
RatesBy weight, with flat fees for mattresses and fridges. Sort your load first: an unsorted load can be charged the mixed rate, double the garbage rate.
HoursOn simcoe.ca and in the Simcoe County Collects app. Closed on statutory holidays unless posted.

Everything else, we load: furniture, appliances and reno waste. Our crew carries it out and hauls it away. Next door: Innisfil and every town we serve.

Treasure Hunt weekends in Bradford West Gwillimbury.

Twice a year, the Town lets residents put things at the curb for anyone to take.

Spring and fallOne weekend each. Dates are on the Town's website.
From 7am SaturdayMark things FREE. The Town has free printable labels.
Gone by 7pm SundayAnything unclaimed comes off the curb, or it may be a bylaw violation.
LeftoversThe Town asks you to donate what is left to charity.

Does Bradford pick up furniture at the curb?

The County of Simcoe does, by appointment and for a fee, through Service Simcoe at 1.800.263.3199. Mattresses and fridges cost extra, and items go out by 7am. For more than a few things, our crew is simpler.

Who does garbage collection in Bradford West Gwillimbury?

The County of Simcoe. The Town sends residents to the County for garbage, and your schedule is on simcoe.ca and in the Simcoe County Collects app.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday 8am to 7pm, and Saturday 8am to 12pm. Closed Sundays and holidays.
