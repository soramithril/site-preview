The fee

What the fee pays for.

We do not post a price, because it depends on two things: how far we drive and how much there is. Tell us what you have and where, and we tell you the price before we book.

The fee covers the pickup from your home, the trip to Furniture Bank and the delivery to a family by our team. The receipt goes on your tax return as a charitable credit, and it can go to you or to anyone you name.

Free ways to pass it on

Looking for free furniture pickup in Barrie?

Our pickup has a fee, so here are the free ways to pass furniture on. If none of them suits you, we come and get it.

Barrie: Curbside Giveaway DaysTwice a year, spring and fall. Put things at the curb marked FREE and take in anything left by 9 pm. The City does not collect furniture at the curb on any other day.
Orillia: The Reuse DepotAt the Waste Diversion Site on Kitchener Street, for gently used furniture and household items.
Bradford: Curbside Treasure HuntA spring and a fall weekend: items at the curb marked FREE.
County: Bulky item pickupTowns on County of Simcoe collection, like Innisfil, can book it. It is paid, by appointment.

Who picks up furniture donations in Barrie?

We do. Jeff's Junk has been Furniture Bank's logistics partner since 2019: our crew picks up from your home, and our team delivers the furniture to families.

Do I get a tax receipt?

Yes. Donors get a charitable tax receipt, and it can go to you or to someone you name on the form. You claim it as a charitable credit on your tax return.

Can I donate a mattress?

Yes, if it is in good shape. A worn mattress can go with our mattress removal.

What if Furniture Bank can't use a piece after pickup?

They check every piece at their warehouse, and if there is a problem they contact you.

Can I add a donation to a junk removal or bin booking?

Yes. When you book, tell us what is still good. Clearing out a parent's house? See estate clean outs.

Where do you pick up donations?

Collingwood, Clearview, Wasaga Beach, Springwater, Tiny, Tay, Midland, Essa, Penetanguishene, Severn, Ramara, Oro-Medonte, Barrie, Innisfil, Bradford West Gwillimbury and New Tecumseth. See our service areas.
