What can't go in a bin?nine things stay out

★★★★★ 5.0 from 260 Google reviews, hauling since 1992

The short answer

Paint, oils and other liquids, chemicals, solvents, asbestos, oil drums, propane tanks, tires and medical waste can't go in any of our bins. Dirt and concrete can go in a bin, but only in their own dirt or concrete bin, never mixed with other junk.

Oils, paint and any other liquids stay out of the bin, even in a sealed can. Outside Barrie, the County of Simcoe runs its own hazardous waste depots. See our Innisfil and Orillia pages for the local sites.

Dirt and concrete: their own bin, no mixing.

NO MIXING. A dirt bin takes dirt only. A concrete bin takes concrete only. No exceptions. If anything else goes in, the whole load has to go to the dump as mixed waste, and you pay for its full weight. Dirt and concrete are so heavy that the bill is very high, so keep it clean. Our dirt and concrete bins are a flat rate with no dump fee.

What can go in a construction dumpster?

Drywall, tile, carpet, wood and demolition debris go straight in a 20 yard. Shingles go in a 14 yard. Tell us about the roof when you book.

What electronics can go in a dumpster?

Call and ask first. Electronics are free at the Barrie landfill for residents, and our junk removal crew takes them.

Is a bin the same as a dumpster?

Yes. Bin, dumpster, garbage bin and disposal bin all mean the same thing here.
