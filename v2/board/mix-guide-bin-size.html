The short answer

Most people rent the 14 yard bin. If it's only dirt, concrete or yard waste, you want the 4 yard, or the 7 yard for a bigger load of dirt or concrete.

What size dumpster should I rent for a house clean out?

The 20 yard. A whole house often takes more than one: most houses we clear take 1 to 6 of our 20 yard bins. Rather not load it? See estate clean outs.

What size dumpster for a garage or basement clean out?

The 14 yard for a garage or a basement clean out. Tearing out a basement in a reno? Take the 20 yard.

Is a bin the same as a dumpster or a garbage bin?

Yes. Bin, dumpster, garbage bin and disposal bin all mean the same thing. Around Barrie most people say bin.

Can I put dirt or concrete in a 14 yard bin?

No. Dirt and concrete go in their own 4 or 7 yard bin, one material per bin, no mixing, no exceptions. A mixed load goes to the dump and is charged by weight.

How long can I keep the bin?

As long as you need. You pick the pickup day. Extend it for a fee, or call and we'll pick it up early.

How soon can you drop off a bin?

Usually the same day you call, even in spring and summer.
