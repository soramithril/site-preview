Appliance disposal in your town.

Here is what the towns we serve allow.

BarrieThe City does not pick up appliances at the curb. The City landfill at 272 Ferndale Drive North takes them from Barrie residents with photo ID. Fridges, freezers, air conditioners and dehumidifiers have an extra fee. Junk removal in Barrie
OrilliaNo bulky pickup at the curb. Fridges, freezers and air conditioners go to the City Waste Diversion Site on Kitchener Street, for a fee. Junk removal in Orillia
Innisfil, Bradford and the other County townsThe County of Simcoe picks appliances up at the curb if you book and pay ahead, with an extra fee for fridges and freezers. It has to be at the curb by 7 a.m., and each item can weigh up to 68 kg (150 lb). The County drop off sites take them too, with proof you live in the County. Junk removal in Innisfil
Buying a new one?Ask the store whether it will take the old one away when it delivers the new one. Some do, and some charge for it.

From the City of Barrie, City of Orillia and County of Simcoe websites, checked October 2026. Rules and fees change, so check with your town before you go.
