Taking it yourself?

Where each town's dump is.

Fees are by weight, with flat fees for things like mattresses and fridges. They change every year, so check before you go.

BarrieThe Barrie Landfill on Ferndale Drive North, for Barrie residents only. Barrie dump hours, fees and what they take
InnisfilNo landfill. Residents use a County of Simcoe waste site, such as Bradford's or Oro's, and cannot use the Barrie landfill. Innisfil page
OrilliaThe Orillia Waste Diversion Site on Kitchener Street, which has a Reuse Depot for good used items. Orillia page
Wasaga BeachNo County site in town. The nearest is the Nottawasaga site in Stayner, about 14 km away. Wasaga Beach page
BradfordThe County's West Gwillimbury waste site on Line 12, with a hazardous waste depot. Bradford page
Other County townsCounty of Simcoe waste sites, with the same fee rules in every town.

Questions

Do you go to Muskoka?

Yes: Gravenhurst, Bracebridge, Huntsville, Port Carling, Bala and around them. Call for the price.

Do you serve York Region?

Yes: Newmarket, Aurora and King City. Call for the price.

Do you serve Simcoe, Ontario?

The town of Simcoe is in Norfolk County, about two hours south of Barrie. We work in Simcoe County, the area around Barrie. Call us and we tell you if we can help.

Do I need a permit for a bin on the road?

It depends on the town. Barrie, Innisfil, Bradford and Wasaga Beach each need a permit for a bin on the road, and the town pages explain each one. A bin on your driveway avoids the road rules.
