No, donation pickup isn't free: there's a pickup fee, and we tell you what it is when you book. In return, your furniture goes to a family in need through Furniture Bank, and you get a charitable tax receipt.

Free options

The free options in Barrie.

Barrie holds Curbside Giveaway Days each spring and fall. Put things at the curb marked "FREE", and bring in anything left by 9 pm. The City doesn't pick up furniture at the curb on other days.
