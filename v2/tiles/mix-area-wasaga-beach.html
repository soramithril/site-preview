A dumpster on a Wasaga Beach road

Main roads need a bigger deposit.

A dumpster fully on your driveway is the usual way. Past your property line, the Town wants a permit.

The Town's Road Occupation Permit covers anything beyond your property line: the boulevard, sidewalk, road or a public lane. Its list names "Disposal bin in roadway or public laneway". On these six main roads, the deposit is twice the size:

  • River Road East
  • River Road West
  • Main Street
  • Mosley Street
  • Sunnidale Road
  • 45th Street South

The permit holder needs $2 million liability insurance naming the Town, and a bin can't fully close a lane. Apply through the Town's CityView portal. Getting a permit is up to you.
