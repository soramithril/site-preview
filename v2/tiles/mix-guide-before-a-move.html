Before you start

The City won't take the big stuff.

The City of Barrie doesn't collect furniture, appliances or renovation waste at the curb. That is why the big items need a plan. Our junk removal in Barrie and bin rental in Barrie cover them.

Your checklist, step by step.

  1. 1
    1. Sort room by roomFour piles: keep, donate, sell, junk. Start with the rooms you use least: basement, garage, spare room.
  2. 2
    2. Give away what still worksGently used furniture can go to a family in need through Furniture Bank. We pick it up for a pickup fee, and you get a charitable tax receipt. See furniture donation pickup in Barrie. Smaller things can go out on one of Barrie's Curbside Giveaway Days, held each spring and fall.

    4. Time the last pickupBook the final pickup for after the movers have loaded what's going, so nothing you need goes by mistake.

What size dumpster do I need for a move?

The 14 yard, about 7 pickup truck loads. Clearing a whole house? The 20 yard. See the bin size guide.

Can you take my junk the day I move out?

Same day junk removal is possible. Call and we'll tell you if we can fit you in.

How do I get an old fridge ready to go?

Empty it, unplug it and let it defrost the day before. A gas line needs a licensed gas technician to disconnect it.

Can I keep the bin until after moving day?

Yes. You choose the pickup day, and you can extend it for a fee.

How far ahead should I book?

Bins usually go out the same day you call. Moving at the end of the month? Call as soon as you know your date.
