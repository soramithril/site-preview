The short answer

A bin is usually the cheaper choice when you can load it yourself and have a few days to fill it. Junk removal is usually better value for a few big or heavy items, or when you'd rather not lift anything, because our crew does all the carrying.

How each one is priced

Two ways to pay.

Junk removal is priced by how much of our 20 yard bin your junk fills, from a small minimum load to a full bin. Some jobs are priced by labour instead. See junk removal prices.A bin rental is a flat price by size. The 14 and 20 yard bins add a dump fee by weight. The 4 and 7 yard dirt and concrete bins don't. Four questions

Four questions that settle it.

Can you lift it all yourself?If not, junk removal.
Do you have a few days?A bin rewards loading at your own pace.
Is there room on the driveway?Every bin is 12 ft long, and the truck needs room to back in.
Is it heavy?Dirt and concrete go in their own bins. Shingles and drywall add weight to the dump fee on a 14 or 20 yard bin.

How much does it cost to rent a dumpster in Barrie?

The 14 yard is $195 for 7 days, or $150 for 3 days, plus the dump fee. The 20 yard is $250 for 1 to 7 days, plus the dump fee. These are Zone 1 prices.

How much does junk removal cost?

Most jobs are $125 to $950, by how much of our 20 yard bin your junk fills.

Is it cheaper to rent a dumpster or go to the dump?

For a small load in your own truck, if you live in Barrie, the dump. For anything bigger, a bin or our crew. See Barrie dump hours and fees.

Which is better for a garage clean out?

A 14 yard bin if you'll clear it over a weekend. Our crew if you want it gone in one visit. A garage is often a full 20 yard load.

Which is better for one couch or a fridge?

Junk removal. One appliance on its own is our minimum, and a couch and a mattress usually comes in close to it.

Do I need a permit for a bin?

Not on your driveway. On the road, you need a permit from your town, and getting it is up to you.

What can't go in a bin?

Paint, oils and other liquids, chemicals, solvents, asbestos, oil drums, propane tanks, tires and medical waste. More on what can't go in a bin.
