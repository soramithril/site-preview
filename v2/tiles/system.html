Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

What can't go in a bin?

Paint, oils, chemicals, solvents, asbestos, oil drums, propane tanks and tires. Dirt and concrete go in their own bin: no mixing, no exceptions.

Is furniture donation pickup free?

No. There is a fee for the pickup. Good furniture goes to Furniture Bank and you get a tax receipt.
