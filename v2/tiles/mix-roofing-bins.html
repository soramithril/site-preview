Can I take shingles to the dump myself?

Yes, the dump takes shingles at its own fee. Our Barrie dump guide has the details. A bin on the driveway saves the trips: the shingles go straight from the roof into the bin.

How long can I keep the bin?

The 14 yard price covers up to 7 days, and you choose the pickup day. Need longer? Extend for a fee, or keep a bin by the month on a long job.

Do I need a permit for the bin?

Not if it goes on your driveway. If it has to sit on the road, you need a road permit from your town, and getting it is up to you.

Is there a weight limit?

On the 14 and 20 yard bins, the dump fee covers up to 1 tonne (2,200 lb) and is prorated after that. A roof heavier than one load gets a swap out.
