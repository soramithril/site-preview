A few minutes before the truck comes

Your driveway, drop off day.

  1. 1
    Pick the spotYour driveway is best. Every bin is 12 ft long. If a car fits in the spot, our truck fits too.
  2. 2
    Clear the wayOn drop off and pickup day, move cars and anything else out of the driveway. Watch for low wires and branches: the truck needs 9 ft of clear height.
  3. 3
    Load it smartFlat, heavy things go in first, then the bulky ones. Break down furniture so it packs tight, spread heavy loads over the whole floor, and close the door before pickup day.

Load it level: nothing above the top edge and nothing sticking out over the sides. An overfilled bin isn't safe to haul, so we can't take it until the load is level.

Is a bin the same as a dumpster or a garbage bin?

Yes. Bin rental, dumpster rental, garbage bin rental and disposal bin rental all mean the same thing; around Barrie most people just say bin.

How soon can you drop off a bin?

Usually the same day you call, even in spring and summer.

How long can I keep my bin?

As long as you need it. You choose the pickup day when you book, and we come and get the bin that day.

Finished early? Give us a call and we'll pick it up sooner. Need more time? You can extend your rental for a fee. Long term rentals are available too.

Do I need a permit for a bin?

Not if it goes on your driveway. If it has to sit on the road, you need a road permit from your town, and getting it is up to you.

What if my bin is too heavy?

On the 14 and 20 yard bins a heavier load costs a little more at the dump, because the dump fee is prorated over 1 tonne. The 4 and 7 yard dirt and concrete bins have no weight limit, as long as the load is clean: one material, nothing mixed in.
