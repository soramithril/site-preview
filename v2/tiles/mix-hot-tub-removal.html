How hot tub removal works.

  1. 1
    Send a photoText a photo of the tub to 705.333.7767, and one of the way out to the driveway. Tell us how we get to it: the gate, the deck, any stairs.
  2. 2
    We cut it up where it sitsWe take the tub apart on site, so it fits through gates and around corners.
  3. 3
    Carried out and hauled awayThe whole tub leaves on our truck in the same visit, and the cover and steps can go with it.

A bigger tub takes longer to cut up and carry out, and the way in matters too: the gate, the side yard, the deck stairs.
